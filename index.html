Exploring how Australians read, what different generations enjoy, and how public libraries are changing in the digital age.

Australian public libraries are evolving. Digital collections have expanded, changing the balance between physical and digital library holdings.

04 — The Changing Library

Registered library membership has declined while recorded collection usage per person has increased. Explore how these two measures changed between 2020 and 2025.

05 — Where Does Australia Read?

Reading habits vary across Australia's states and territories. This map shows the percentage of Australians aged 15 and over who read books for pleasure in 2025. Darker green represents higher reading participation.

Source: Creative Australia, National Arts Participation Survey 2025. Figures are survey estimates.

06 — Where Are Australia's Public Libraries?

Public libraries provide access to books and digital resources across Australia. This map compares the number of reported public library service points in each state and territory during 2024–25. Larger circles represent more service points.

Note: Circles are positioned within each state for comparison, not at individual library locations. Counts include branches, mobile libraries and other outlets.

Source: ALIA/NSLA, Australian Public Libraries Statistical Report 2024–25.

Sources: Creative Australia (NAPS 2025) and ALIA/NSLA (2024–25).
